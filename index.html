About us

Pristine Marketing Group (PMG) is a full-service event organizer based in Addis Ababa. We plan, produce, and promote concerts and cultural events — handling everything from venue booking and artist logistics to ticketing and marketing, so the only thing our clients and audiences need to think about is showing up.

Our team has worked with musicians, cultural institutions, and venues across the city to put together events that feel unmistakably Ethiopian, built with local audiences and traditions at the center.
